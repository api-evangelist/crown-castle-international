# Crown Castle (crown-castle-international)

Crown Castle is a leading United States provider of shared communications infrastructure including cell towers, small cells, and fiber. Crown Castle owns and operates more than 40,000 cell towers and approximately 90,000 route miles of fiber, supporting wireless carriers, enterprises, and government customers with managed connectivity, dark fiber, optical encryption, ultra-low-latency transport, and managed SD-WAN services. Crown Castle does not currently publish a public developer API program; technical integrations are handled through customer and channel partner agreements.
